Tidy Tab Groups

Group names

One per line: address = Name. A group is named after the site you opened the link from. Your lines come first, then a built-in list of well-known sites, then the plain domain. An address also covers its subdomains: example.com matches shop.example.com. Add a path to name just part of a site, like facebook.com/messages = Messenger. If several lines fit a page, the most specific address wins (the longer domain, then the longer path). If two are equally specific, the lower line wins.
